How to Send

Send an ISO 20022 pacs.008 payment to either IBAN above. The container receives it, posts the credit, and returns a camt.054 confirmation automatically.

Receive endpoint:

POST http://[2001:470:1f27:a5::1]:42881/receive
Content-Type: application/xml
Body: pacs.008.001.10 XML

What Happens on Receipt

  1. Payment received and coherence-validated
  2. Credit posted to your account for that currency
  3. camt.054 credit notification returned immediately
  4. Transaction recorded in ledger with ByteID
